Home gymnastics is strength and play. It is not YouTube’s worst idea on hardwood. Clear a mat-sized rectangle. That’s the gym.

## The circuit (12 minutes)

1. Bear crawl to the couch and back
2. Frog jumps, ten
3. Wall walk toward a handstand — **use a spotter, stop when arms shake**
4. Hollow hold, count of ten, twice
5. Forward roll on a mat only

## Hard no

- Furniture as a vault
- Beds as a tumble track
- “Watch this” without a spotter

End with a long stretch and water. If anyone wants more, draw a floor routine on paper. The carpet is retired for the day.
