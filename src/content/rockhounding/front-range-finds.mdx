You do not need a claim and a rock hammer the size of a femur. You need a legal public area, a small bag, and a finish line. Pikes Peak region sites, some BLM spots, and pay-to-dig days all work — **check current rules** before you put a pebble in a pocket.

## Effort, for real

- Walk: under a mile unless the group begs
- Sun: brutal. Hats or you go home early.
- Time: 90 minutes of looking is a full shift

## The keep-one rule

Each person picks one favorite rock to take home if the site allows collecting. Everything else gets photographed and left. That rule keeps the backpack honest and the site from looking strip-mined.

## What to pack

- Small bags, not a five-gallon bucket
- Water, sunscreen, closed-toe shoes
- A field guide or a phone photo of “what we hope to see”
- Wet wipes. Dirt is the whole sport.

If collecting is closed that day, you still win: name the rocks, sketch them, leave them. The hunt is the day.
