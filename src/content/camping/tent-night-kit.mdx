Colorado camping is not “it’s summer, we’re fine.” The sun leaves and the temperature files for divorce. Pack for the night you will actually get.

## In the kit, always

- Sleeping bags rated colder than the forecast
- Fleece or puffy in the sleeping bag, not in the car
- Wool socks dedicated to sleep. Day socks do not get a second job.
- A pee plan: bottle, walk, or “we are putting shoes on”
- Earplugs for the person who hears every zipper

## Leave in the car

- The extra toys. They become gravel.
- The giant stove if you already have a backup breakfast

If someone is shivering, you add a layer and a snack before you add a lecture. Warm people sleep. Cold people narrate the entire forest.
