Do not start with a 14er trailhead and a three-mile pack-in. A first car-camp is a reserved site you can drive to, a bathroom you can find in the dark, and one meal that is not a science project.

## Effort, for real

- Drive: under two hours from Denver if you can swing it
- Walk from car to tent: you should still be speaking to each other
- Night: one. Two nights is a sequel, not a debut.

## What to pack

- The fat sleeping pads. Nobody “toughs it out” well at 2 a.m.
- Headlamps on every person, plus one extra
- A layer warmer than you think, even in July
- A breakfast you can eat if the stove sulks

## The move

Set the tent before anyone is hungry. Eat early. Walk the loop once so everyone knows where the bathroom is. Lights out is a suggestion until 9, then it is a law. If someone wants the car at 1 a.m., the car is allowed. You still camped.
