People do not always say “I feel weird.” They get quiet, cranky, or dramatic about socks. Treat water and manners as the same lesson: take care of your body, then take care of everyone else on the path.

## Altitude, short version

- Sleep low, play a little higher, go home
- Headache + nausea + “I want to lie down” = turn around, no debate
- Sugar helps moods. Water helps brains.

## Water math that works

Give everyone a bottle they can empty. When it is empty, you refill it. “Sip every time we stop” beats “remember to hydrate.”

## Trail manners in three lines

- Uphill people get the lane
- Voices stay smaller than the creek
- Snacks stay in hands, not on the ground — chipmunks already have a union

If you pack out a stranger’s wrapper, you are doing it right. That is the whole flex.
