You do not need a ski pass for a good snow day. A Front Range sled hill — Genesee, a neighborhood park after a storm, or a gentle ranch hill — is enough if you treat warmth like a project.

## Effort, for real

- Walk from car to hill should be under five minutes
- One person at the top, one watching the runout
- Quit while cheeks are still pink, not blue

## What to pack

- Waterproof mittens, plus a dry backup pair
- A foam sled or something you can steer. Saucers are chaos.
- Thermos cocoa. This is not optional.
- A dry bag for soaked layers so the car does not become a swamp

## The rule

Ten good runs beat thirty grim ones. When the first person says they are done, you are done. The hill will still be there after the next storm.
