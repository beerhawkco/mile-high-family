The enemy of game night is the second game and the person who “just wants to explain the full rules.” Pick one box you can teach in three minutes. If the table is mixed, make it cooperative so nobody gets voted off the island.

## What works

- Short rounds, obvious turns
- A snack that is not also a game piece
- A timer you honor. When it dings, you are done — even mid-quest.

## What does not

- 90-minute epics “they’ll grow into”
- House rules invented mid-turn
- Screens on the table “just for the score app”

Put the box away together. That is the ritual. Tomorrow someone will ask for the same game. That is the win.
