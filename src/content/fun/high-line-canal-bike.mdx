The High Line Canal is the anti-traffic bike day. Pick a segment with bathrooms and a turnaround. Cherry Hills, Littleton, and parts of Aurora all work. The canal is the path. You are not obligated to ride 71 miles.

## Effort, for real

- Surface: mostly packed dirt or gravel — fat tires help
- Distance: three miles out and back is enough for a mixed group
- Shade: better than a street ride, still bring sun stuff

## Make it a loop

1. Start at a park you already like
2. Ride to a bridge, watch the water, turn around
3. End with a cold drink in the parking lot

Helmets on. Bell if you have one. Yield to walkers like you are the guest — because you are.
