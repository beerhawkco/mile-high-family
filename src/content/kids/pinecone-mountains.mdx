Collect pinecones on a neighborhood walk. Do not raid a park display. At home, you build mountains.

## You need

- Pinecones, dry
- A cardboard base
- Glue that will actually hold (school glue works if you wait)
- White paint or cotton for “snow”
- Optional: tiny flags on toothpicks

## Steps

1. Glue cones in a line, tallest in the middle
2. Dab snow on the tips only — kids will paint the whole cone anyway
3. Name the peaks. Yes, “Mount Snack” counts.
4. Let it dry while you eat the actual snack

This is a 4–6 craft. If a 9-year-old wants in, they can draw a trail map on the cardboard and make up the difficulty rating.
