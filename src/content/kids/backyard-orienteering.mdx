Draw a terrible map of the yard or a nearby park. Hide three things. Kids navigate. You do not hover unless a street is involved.

## Setup, 15 minutes

- Sketch the space from above — mailbox, tree, porch, weird rock
- Mark start with a star
- Hide a water bottle, a note, and a “summit snack”
- Give each kid a card with one compass word: north is the street, or the alley, or the big tree. Pick one landmark and stick to it.

## Rules

- Walk. This is not a tackle sport.
- If you find someone else’s clue, leave it
- Last stop is always food

Add a timer only if they ask. The win is reading the map, not sprinting. If you are in a public park, keep hides obvious and pick them up when you leave.
